# Writing Style Guide — cornaciu.ro

Guidelines for writing Romanian legal website text, derived from analyzing [ea-avocat.ro](https://ea-avocat.ro) and [piperea.ro](https://www.piperea.ro).

---

## Tone & Voice

1. **Formal but warm.** Use "dumneavoastră" consistently. Be professional without being cold — confident authority with approachable warmth.
2. **Confident, not boastful.** State credentials and results as facts: *"Cu peste 35 de ani de experiență..."* — never *"Suntem cei mai buni..."*
3. **Client-centered.** Always frame services from the client's perspective: what *they* get, what problem *they* solve.
4. **Third-person institutional voice for the firm** (*"Cabinetul oferă..."*, *"Vă asigurăm..."*), with occasional first-person plural (*"Abordarea noastră..."*).

---

## Language Rules

5. **Use standard legal Romanian vocabulary:**
   - *consultanță juridică*, *asistență juridică și reprezentare*
   - *instanțe de judecată*, *acțiune în instanță*
   - *cerere de chemare în judecată*, *întâmpinare*
   - *executare silită*, *clauze abuzive*, *creanțe*
   - *despăgubiri*, *daune morale*

6. **Prefer active constructions:** *"Oferim consultanță"*, *"Vă asistăm și vă reprezentăm"*, *"Redactăm cereri"* — not passive *"Consultanța este oferită de cabinet"*.

7. **Start service descriptions with action verbs:** *Oferim*, *Asigurăm*, *Vă sprijinim*, *Redactăm*, *Ne ocupăm de*, *Vă stăm la dispoziție*.

8. **Use connector words for flow:** *De asemenea*, *Totodată*, *În plus*, *Mai mult*, *În acest sens*.

9. **Don't use English loanwords** where Romanian equivalents exist (*fuziuni* not *mergers*).

---

## Sentence Construction

10. **Mix sentence lengths.** Lead with a short authoritative statement, follow with a longer explanatory sentence.
11. **One idea per sentence.** Don't stack three subordinate clauses. If a sentence exceeds ~35 words, break it.
12. **Use enumerations sparingly in prose.** Bullet lists for service pages are fine. For articles and "about" sections, use flowing paragraphs.

---

## Content Patterns

### Service Description Formula
- Open with a 1–2 sentence authority statement about the practice area
- List specific services using bullet points starting with action verbs
- Close with reassurance: *"Un specialist în [domeniu] vă stă la dispoziție cu soluții juridice personalizate și eficiente."*

### Article / Blog Post Formula
- Descriptive, keyword-rich title — state the legal topic clearly
- Opening paragraph that frames the problem from the client's viewpoint: *"Vă confruntați cu...?"* or *"În practica juridică actuală..."*
- Body structured with numbered or subtitled sections
- Precise legal references (article numbers, law names)
- Closing CTA linking back to a consultation

### About / Bio Formula
- Lead with years of experience and education credentials
- Mention institutional affiliations (bar membership, certifications)
- Briefly describe practice philosophy in 2–3 sentences
- Highlight notable results without naming clients

## Formatting Conventions

- **Section labels:** ALL CAPS (*DOMENII DE PRACTICĂ*, *ARII DE EXPERTIZĂ*)
- **Headings:** Title Case in Romanian (*Drept Civil*, *Dreptul Familiei*)
- **Legal references:** Full formal citation — *"Legea nr. 77/2016 privind darea în plată..."*
- **Address form:** Always *dumneavoastră*, never *tu/tine*

---

## What NOT to Do

- Colloquial Romanian or slang
- First-person confessional or spiritual tone
- Walls of unbroken academic text with 50+ word sentences
- Unsubstantiated superlative claims (*"cel mai bun cabinet din România"*)
- Informal address (*tu/tine*)
